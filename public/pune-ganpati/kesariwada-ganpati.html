Kesariwada Ganpati, Pune

Kesariwada Ganpati is a Manache Paach Ganpati in Narayan Peth, Pune. Address: Kesari Wada, 568, Narayan Peth, Pune, Maharashtra 411030. Timings: 08:00 AM - 10:00 PM. Nearest metro: PMC Metro Station (700m).

The 5th Manacha Ganpati. Lokmanya Bal Gangadhar Tilak started public Ganeshotsav here in 1894 to unite people during the Indian freedom struggle.
