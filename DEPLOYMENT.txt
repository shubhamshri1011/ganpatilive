DEPLOYMENT CHECKLIST

1. Replace your GitHub index.html with the included index.html.
2. Put robots.txt and sitemap.xml in the repository root.
3. Put llms.txt in the repository root.
4. Deploy to Vercel.
5. Confirm https://www.puneganpatilive.in/ is the canonical domain. If Vercel uses non-www as primary, change canonical/sitemap/llms references consistently.
6. In Google Search Console, inspect the homepage and request indexing, then submit /sitemap.xml.
7. Do not create fake/hidden backlinks. Real backlinks must come from external websites, local directories, social profiles, blogs, news sites, colleges, mandal pages, or business partners that choose to link to the site.
